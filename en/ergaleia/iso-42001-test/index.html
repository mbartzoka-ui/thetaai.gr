How ready is your company for ISO 42001?

Ten questions, three minutes, no sign-up. See where you stand against the AI management system standard and which gaps to close first. Nothing is stored.

1. Do you have an inventory of every AI system the company uses, including tools like ChatGPT that employees use?
2. Is there a written AI policy approved by management?
3. Is someone named as accountable for each AI system?
4. Do you assess AI risks with a documented method?
5. Do you assess the impact of AI systems on the people they affect (customers, staff, candidates)?
6. Do you check your AI suppliers: what the contract says, where data goes, what happens when the model changes?
7. Do you know and document which data your AI systems use or "see"?
8. Do you monitor how AI systems behave in practice and log errors or incidents?
9. Have staff been trained in correct and safe use of AI?
10. Do you already have an ISO 27001 or ISO 9001 system or certificate?
Please answer every question.
